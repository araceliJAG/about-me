### 🧭 My Journey: First-Gen Grit, Resilience & Finding My Path

#### 🎓 Forging the Path as a First-Generation Trailblazer
As the oldest of four siblings in a working-class household, there was no pre-written roadmap for college or career building. Early on, I realized that if I wanted to open doors for myself and my family, I had to build them from scratch:
* **Academic Discipline:** Without transportation to attend after-school clubs, I poured my focus entirely into academics—graduating high school with honors and a 3.7 GPA.
* **Overcoming the Financial Maze:** Navigating admissions, FAFSA, and financial aid completely on my own brought tough, formative lessons. After initially paying out-of-pocket at Oglethorpe University based on misleading guidance—enduring a grueling 3-hour daily bus commute while working—I took complete ownership of my academic and financial strategy.
* **Pivoting with Purpose:** When my original plan in forensic science clashed with the realities of medical school debt and schedule constraints, I refused to stay stuck. I transferred to GSU Perimeter College, earned my Associate degree in Business, and advanced to Georgia State University’s Atlanta campus to study Computer Information Systems (CIS) in a program ranked among the Top 10 nationally.

#### 🩺 Foundations in Healthcare Science & Clinical Discipline
Long before discovering my passion for information systems and cybersecurity, my childhood dream was rooted in science, forensic analysis, and patient care. Rather than waiting for college, I took the initiative in high school to turn that curiosity into certified, hands-on clinical credentials:
* **Accelerated Dual Enrollment Pathway:** Beginning in 10th grade, I enrolled in a rigorous, two-year healthcare science dual-enrollment program through Gwinnett Technical College, balancing college-level coursework alongside my standard curriculum.
* **Advanced Medical & Anatomical Coursework:** Completed collegiate training in:
  * **ALHS 1011 - Structure & Function of the Human Body:** Intensive human anatomy, physiology, microbiology, and pathophysiological analysis.
  * **Introduction to Healthcare Science:** Fundamental clinical skills, emergency response (Basic Life Support/First Aid), medical ethics, and patient communication.
  * **Essentials of Healthcare:** Medical-focused physiology, clinical terminology, and diagnostic intervention analysis.
* **Patient Care Technician (PCT) Certification:** Successfully passed the state and national board examinations to earn my official PCT certification. Through clinical simulations, I mastered strict compliance standards enforced by regulatory bodies including OSHA, CDC, HHS, OBRA 1987, and **HIPAA** patient privacy laws.
* **The Bridge to Systems & Security:** While I ultimately fell in love with business systems and data security, this clinical foundation directly shaped my analytical rigor, attention to detail, and deep respect for data privacy and regulatory compliance.

#### 💼 Five Years of Cross-Functional Retail Operations
* **Versatile Store Operations:** Starting as a cashier during my senior year of high school, I have built 5+ years of continuous service with Kroger. Currently in the Pick-Up (ClickList) department, I balance a demanding 24–32 hours per week alongside full-time university studies.
* **Cross-Department Adaptability:** Beyond front-end customer checkout and e-commerce fulfillment, I have cross-trained and supported nearly every primary area of the store—including Produce, Meat/Seafood, Floral, Deli, Bakery, Grocery, and Drug/GM. Whenever unexpected bottlenecks emerge, I step in seamlessly to keep daily operations moving.
* **The "Older Sibling" Mindset:** Growing up as the trailblazer taught me how to handle life's most confusing administrative hurdles—filing personal and business taxes, handling healthcare paperwork, submitting FAFSA, and budgeting. I carry that exact resilience and proactive care into every project team and workplace.
